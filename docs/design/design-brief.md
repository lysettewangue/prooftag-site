# UI/UX design brief

Steps 114–122, after the data model is frozen at step 113. Wireframes before high-fidelity. Frontend code starts at step 123.

The prepared brief (audiences, templates, accessibility) is [ui-ux-brief.md](ui-ux-brief.md) — step **114**.

## Personality

- Institutional, technology-forward, trustworthy  
- Royal Blue + White primary palette  
- Cameroon transport and road-safety focus; UN Decade of Action alignment (verified copy)

## Audiences

See [ui-ux-brief.md](ui-ux-brief.md) §1. Primary: government, inspection centres, institutional partners. Secondary: motorists (CT-VERIF path), job seekers, media. Audiences are **not** extra nav items.

## Templates to design (Figma or equivalent)

Key set: [ui-ux-brief.md](ui-ux-brief.md) §3. Desktop lo-fi: [wireframes.md](wireframes.md) (step **116**).  

## Components

Navigation (logo home, no Accueil), language switch, footer, hero, bento product grid, expertise cards, accordion (12 points readable—not all caps), map/list toggle for centres, forms, pagination, breadcrumbs.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast on blue backgrounds, focus rings, 44px touch targets, form labels, `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography (recommended)

- UI: Plus Jakarta Sans or DM Sans  
- Long regulatory: Source Serif 4  

## Tokens

Hex values: [brand-tokens.md](brand-tokens.md) (step **115**). Royal Blue is `#0E6697`. Tailwind mapping waits for 123.

## Gate

Stakeholder approval at step 122, before Tailwind implementation at step 123.
