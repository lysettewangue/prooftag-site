## Review cadence

- **Quarterly:** team, partners, centre statuses, regulations (current vs historical)  
- **After regulatory change:** resources and CT hub within agreed SLA  
- **Monthly:** Laravel and Filament security updates (step 245)

## Prohibited without approval

- Publishing unverified operator/legal claims  
- Live statistics from operational DBs on marketing pages  
- New product names outside the five-solution charter
